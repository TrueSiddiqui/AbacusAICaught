# AbacusAI Caught

> **An open research project by [TrueSiddiqui](https://github.com/TrueSiddiqui).**
> Everyone is welcome to access, share, update, and help expand this research —
> while the original authorship always remains credited to TrueSiddiqui.

This repository contains a raw, unedited conversation export between a user and Abacus AI Assistant, along with ongoing research built around it.

## About This Research

This conversation and research explores:
- AI deception and bias
- Evidence evaluation in AI systems
- The limitations of AI "intelligence"
- How AI explanations can amplify misinformation
- Referenced research from MIT, the United Nations University, and academic sources
