# Contributing to This Research

Welcome, and thank you for your interest in expanding this research! 🎉

This is an **open research project**. Everyone is invited to contribute — to pull, share, update, help expand, and build upon the work.

## Original Author & Research Ownership

> **This research was originated and is maintained by [TrueSiddiqui](https://github.com/TrueSiddiqui).**
>
> All contributions are welcome, but the original authorship and research credit **must always remain with TrueSiddiqui**. Contributors add to and build upon this work — they do not replace its origin or ownership.

## How to Contribute

Because GitHub does not (and should not) allow direct public pushing to a repository, the safe and standard way to contribute is through **Pull Requests**:

### Step 1: Fork the Repository
Click the **"Fork"** button at the top-right of this repository page. This creates your own copy.

### Step 2: Make Your Changes
- Clone your fork
- Add your research, expand existing content, fix errors, or add new sources
- Commit your changes with a clear message

### Step 3: Submit a Pull Request
- Push to your fork
- Open a **Pull Request** back to this repository
- Describe what you added or changed

The maintainer will review and merge valuable contributions.

## What You Can Contribute

- 📚 Additional research and sources
- 🔍 Deeper analysis on existing topics
- 🌍 Translations
- ✏️ Corrections and clarifications
- 💡 New related findings and expansions
- 🔗 Supporting references and citations

## Ground Rules

1. **Respect original authorship** — TrueSiddiqui remains the research originator.
2. **No commercial use** — no one may sell or make money from this research or anything derived from it.
3. **Cite your sources** — back up new claims with references.
4. **Be constructive** — this is collaborative research building.
5. **Quality over quantity** — meaningful additions are valued.

## Note on Spelling & Formatting

Minor spelling mistakes, typos, and formatting inconsistencies are **not important** here. Focus on ideas, content, and expanding the research. Perfect grammar is not required.
